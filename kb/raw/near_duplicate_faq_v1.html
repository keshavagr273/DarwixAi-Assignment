How can I pay my insurance premium online?

To pay your insurance premium online, visit the official Meridian QuickPay web portal at pay.meridianassure.com. Enter your 10-digit policy number and date of birth to view your outstanding premium amount. You can complete the transaction using UPI, debit card, credit card, or internet banking. Once the payment is verified, an instant digital receipt and updated policy renewal certificate will be sent directly to your registered email address.
