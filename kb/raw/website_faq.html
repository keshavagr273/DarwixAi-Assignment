Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Meridian Shield cashless claim process?

Policyholders can obtain cashless hospital admission at over 8,500 network hospitals nationwide. Show your digital Meridian Health Card at the hospital TPA desk at least 48 hours prior to planned hospitalisation or within 24 hours of emergency admission. The pre-authorisation request is processed within 60 minutes.

Can I add my parents to my existing health policy?

Yes, senior citizen parents up to age 75 may be enrolled under the Meridian Family Floater Rider during the annual policy renewal window. A nominal medical screening test is mandatory for parents aged 60 and above.
