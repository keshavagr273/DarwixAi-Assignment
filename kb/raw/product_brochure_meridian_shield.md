# MERIDIAN SHIELD HEALTH PROTECTION BROCHURE
## Comprehensive Family & Individual Medical Cover

### Product Overview
Meridian Shield is our flagship health insurance plan providing sum insured options from ₹5 Lakhs to ₹50 Lakhs. It covers inpatient hospitalisation, day care treatments, ambulance costs, organ donor expenses, and annual health checkups.

### Free-Look Period
Policyholders have a period of 15 days from receipt of the policy document to review the terms. If dissatisfied, the policy may be cancelled with full refund minus stamp duty and proportionate risk premium.
