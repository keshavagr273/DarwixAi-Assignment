# MERIDIAN ASSURE — NUDGE & COMPLIANCE PLAYBOOK
## Real-Time Call Nudge Guidelines and Approved Texts (Q4)

> Strict Rule: All compliance and behavioral nudges delivered to human advisors must not exceed 18 words.

### 1. Mandatory Compliance Disclosures (P0)
- **Call Recording Consent:**
  - *Trigger:* Within the first 25 seconds of an answered call.
  - *Approved Nudge Text:* "Mandatory: Inform customer this call is recorded for quality and regulatory compliance." (12 words)
- **Grace Period Accuracy Notice:**
  - *Trigger:* Advisor mentions grace period or policy lapse terms.
  - *Approved Nudge Text:* "Clarify: 30-day grace period applies for annual renewal; full coverage remains active." (12 words)
- **Statutory Free-Look Right:**
  - *Trigger:* Customer agrees to new policy issuance or upgraded rider.
  - *Approved Nudge Text:* "Disclose 15-day free-look cancellation window with 100% premium refund rights." (10 words)

### 2. Escalation & Frustration Triggers (P1)
- **Customer Anger / Threat of Regulatory Complaint:**
  - *Trigger:* Customer mentions "Ombudsman", "Lawyer", "Consumer Court", or elevated acoustic pitch.
  - *Approved Nudge Text:* "De-escalate immediately. Empathize with customer frustration and offer senior supervisor transfer." (11 words)
- **Vulnerability / Financial Hardship:**
  - *Trigger:* Customer mentions bereavement, job loss, or serious hospitalization.
  - *Approved Nudge Text:* "Acknowledge hardship with empathy. Offer quarterly payment schedule or hardship grace extension." (12 words)

### 3. Cross-Sell & Opportunity Nudges (P2)
- **Multi-Vehicle / Hospital Cash Cross-Sell:**
  - *Trigger:* Customer affirms family renewal commitment.
  - *Approved Nudge Text:* "Customer eligible for Hospital Cash rider at ₹49/month with 20% family floater discount." (13 words)
- **Digital Auto-Pay Enrollment:**
  - *Trigger:* Customer asks about payment methods.
  - *Approved Nudge Text:* "Offer UPI AutoPay enrollment for instant 5% cashback on future renewals." (11 words)
