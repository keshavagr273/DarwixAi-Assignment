# MERIDIAN ASSURE — OBJECTION HANDLING PLAYBOOK
## For Voice & Branch Customer Care Advisors

### Objection 1: "The premium is too high this year"
- **Empathy & Validation:** "I completely understand managing annual expenses can feel heavy, especially right now."
- **Value Anchor:** "Your Meridian Shield policy currently holds ₹5,00,000 in sum insured, plus you've accumulated a 20% Cumulative No-Claim Bonus (additional ₹1,00,000 cover at zero extra cost). If the policy lapses, that accrued bonus resets to zero."
- **Alternative Option:** "We can switch your billing frequency to quarterly or semi-annual payments to reduce your immediate out-of-pocket payment today."

### Objection 2: "I didn't make any claims last year, so this policy is a waste of money"
- **Empathy & Validation:** "It's wonderful that you and your family stayed healthy throughout the past twelve months."
- **Value Anchor:** "Insurance is protection for unexpected medical emergencies. Moreover, because you made no claims, your No-Claim Bonus increased your coverage, and your Pre-Existing Disease waiting period is now 12 months closer to full waiver."
- **Health Checkup Reminder:** "Don't forget you also have a complimentary annual health checkup voucher worth ₹2,500 that you can utilize this week."

### Objection 3: "I want to switch to a competitor insurer"
- **Portability Protocol:** "You have the legal right to port your policy to any IRDAI-registered insurer under portability regulations. However, portability applications must be submitted at least 45 days prior to your renewal date."
- **Risk Disclosure:** "If you let this policy lapse now to start fresh elsewhere, your 36-month pre-existing disease waiting period clock resets to zero at the new insurer."

### Objection 4: "I don't have the money right now, call me back next month"
- **Grace Period Clarification:** "You don't need to risk losing your cover today. Under IRDAI guidelines, your policy has a full 30-day grace period from your due date during which you remain fully covered."
- **Commitment:** "Shall I schedule an automated SMS payment link for the 25th day of your grace period, or would you like to break this into quarterly instalments?"
