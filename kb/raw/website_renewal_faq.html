Renewal Payment Guidelines & Timelines

How many days do I have to pay my renewal before my policy lapses?

Meridian Assure allows a grace period of 15 days from your renewal date to complete your premium payment. If payment is not received within 15 days, your policy will lapse immediately and benefits will terminate.

Can I renew my policy online?

Yes. Policyholders can renew their policy via net banking, UPI, debit/credit cards, or mobile wallets by entering their 10-digit policy number on the Meridian QuickPay portal.
