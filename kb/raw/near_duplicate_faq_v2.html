How do I make my insurance premium payment online?

To pay your insurance premium online, please go to the official Meridian QuickPay web portal at pay.meridianassure.com. Simply enter your 10-digit policy number along with your date of birth to check your pending premium due. You may finalize the transaction using UPI, debit card, credit card, or net banking. Once your transaction is confirmed, an instant payment receipt and renewed certificate will be emailed directly to your registered email account.
