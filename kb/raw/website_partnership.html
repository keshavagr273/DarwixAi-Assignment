Branch Partnership Benefits

Empowering banking partners with streamlined bancassurance products and co-located servicing.

Overview

Meridian Assure offers dedicated branch partnership privileges to institutional banking networks across tier-1 and tier-2 locations. Under this agreement, partner bank branches serve as certified distribution nodes for health, life, and credit-shield insurance solutions.

Key Partner Privileges

  • Reciprocal Referral Fees: Bank branches earn an upfront referral commission of 12.5% on all primary health and hospital cash policy issuances routed through official partner desks.
  • Co-Located Claims Concierge: High-volume branches receive a dedicated Meridian Claims Liaison officer on-site to assist policyholders with cashless pre-authorization and instant claim settlement documentation within 48 hours.
  • Instant Digital Endorsements: Core banking integration enables immediate real-time policy issuance via API, eliminating paper documentation and shortening turnaround to under 90 seconds.
  • Dedicated Partner Escalation Hotline: Direct priority telephony routing (1800-PARTNER-ASSURE) connecting branch managers to Senior Underwriting Directors.

Eligibility and Compliance

Branch eligibility requires maintaining a valid corporate agency license under IRDAI guidelines, alongside mandatory bi-annual compliance training for all customer-facing relationship managers.
