# MERIDIAN ASSURE — OFFICIAL POLICY WORDING
## Comprehensive Hospital Cash & Surgical Protection Plan (UIN: MER-HC-2024-V2)

### Section 1: Scope of Coverage
The Company agrees, subject to terms, exclusions, and conditions herein contained, to pay a Daily Hospital Cash Benefit in the event of continuous hospitalisation exceeding 24 hours due to covered illness or accidental bodily injury.

### Section 2: Daily Allowance Benefit Schedule
1. **Standard Inpatient Care:** ₹3,000 per day up to a cumulative maximum of 90 days per policy year.
2. **Intensive Care Unit (ICU):** ₹6,000 per day (2x standard allowance) up to a cumulative maximum of 15 days per policy year.
3. **Convalescence Allowance:** Lump sum ₹10,000 in the event of hospitalisation exceeding 10 consecutive days.

### Section 3: Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period
Coverage for Pre-Existing Diseases commences only after thirty-six (36) months of continuous coverage from the initial policy inception date, provided renewal premiums have been remitted without lapse.

### Section 4: Premium Payment and Grace Period
#### Section 4.1 Payment Mode
Premiums may be paid annually, half-yearly, or quarterly via authorised electronic channels (NEFT, UPI, Direct Debit, Credit Card).

#### Section 4.2 Grace Period (Statutory IRDAI Rule)
A grace period of thirty (30) calendar days from the scheduled due date shall be allowed for payment of yearly, half-yearly, and quarterly premiums. For monthly payment frequencies, the grace period shall be fifteen (15) days. During the thirty (30) day grace period, the policy insurance coverage shall remain continuously in force, and valid hospitalisation claims arising during this window shall be honored subject to deduction of overdue premium.

### Section 5: Revival of Lapsed Policies
If premium is not remitted before the expiry of the 30-day grace period, the policy lapses. A lapsed policy may be revived within six (6) months of the due date upon submission of a Good Health Declaration and remittance of all unpaid dues plus a nominal revival charge.
